  - question: "How long does it take to incorporate a Private Limited Company or LLP in Uttar Pradesh?"
    answer: "Once Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and name approval prerequisites are ready, filing the integrated SPICe+ (INC-32) form with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (Central Registration Centre / ROC Kanpur jurisdiction) typically takes 5 to 10 working days, subject to document verification and PAN/TAN issuance."
  - question: "Are non-compete clauses after termination of employment enforceable in India?"
    answer: "Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, post-termination non-compete clauses that restrict an individual from exercising a lawful profession or trade are generally void. However, in-term exclusivity clauses, confidentiality obligations, intellectual property assignments, and narrowly tailored non-solicitation covenants remain enforceable."
  - question: "What is required to make a commercial contract legally enforceable in Uttar Pradesh?"
    answer: "Under Section 10 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, an agreement requires free consent of competent parties, lawful consideration, and a lawful object. Additionally, to be admissible in evidence without penalty under the Indian Stamp Act as applicable in Uttar Pradesh, the instrument must be executed on appropriate UP e-Stamp paper."
  - question: "Does Zodiac Legal assist with ongoing post-incorporation ROC and MSME compliance?"
    answer: "Yes. Following incorporation, entities must file Form INC-20A (declaration of commencement of business within 180 days), appoint a statutory auditor within 30 days, maintain statutory registers, and complete annual AOC-4 and MGT-7 filings with ROC Kanpur."
