  - question: "What are the strict statutory timelines for a Section 138 cheque bounce case?"
    answer: "First, the cheque must be presented within 3 months of its date (validity period). Second, upon dishonour, the payee must issue a written statutory demand notice within 30 days of receiving the bank return memo. Third, the drawer has 15 days from receipt of notice to make payment. Fourth, if unpaid, the criminal complaint must be filed before the jurisdictional Magistrate within 30 days after the 15-day cure period expires."
  - question: "Where are UP-RERA complaints for Lucknow real estate projects heard?"
    answer: "Complaints regarding registered real estate projects in Lucknow and adjoining districts are filed online through the UP-RERA portal and heard at the UP-RERA Bench in Lucknow (Naveen Bhawan, Rajya Niyojan Sansthan, Kalakankar House Road, Old Hyderabad, Lucknow). Appeals lie before the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Appellate Tribunal (REAT) in Lucknow."
  - question: "What is the minimum default threshold to initiate insolvency (CIRP) before NCLT Allahabad?"
    answer: "Under Section 4 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (as amended), the minimum amount of default required for a financial creditor (Section 7) or operational creditor (Section 9) to file a Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process application before NCLT Allahabad Bench is Rs. 1 Crore."
  - question: "Can an MSME in Uttar Pradesh recover delayed payments without filing a regular civil suit?"
    answer: "Yes. Under Sections 15 to 18 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006, a registered Micro or Small enterprise can file a claim on the MSME Samadhaan portal before the Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council (MSEFC) for principal plus compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate."
