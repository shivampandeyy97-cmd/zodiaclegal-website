  - question: 'Does submitting the website inquiry form create an advocate-client relationship?'
    answer: 'No. In compliance with Bar Council of India Rules, submitting an inquiry through this website or communicating preliminary facts for a conflict-of-interest check does not establish an advocate-client relationship. Formal representation begins only upon execution of a written engagement letter or vakalatnama.'
  - question: 'What documents should I bring to an initial legal consultation at Zodiac Legal?'
    answer: 'Please prepare a chronological summary of dates and events, copies of executed agreements or purchase orders, relevant statutory notices or show-cause letters received, bank return memos or ledger statements (for recovery matters), and any prior court or tribunal pleadings.'
  - question: 'Can businesses across India engage Zodiac Legal for nationwide corporate matters?'
    answer: 'Yes. We regularly advise domestic corporations, startups, promoters, and creditors located across India for corporate governance, commercial contracts, RERA proceedings, and litigation before High Courts, Commercial Courts, NCLT, and DRT benches nationwide.'
  - question: 'How are legal consultation and representation fees structured?'
    answer: 'Fee structures depend on the nature of the mandate—such as fixed-scope transactional drafting, monthly corporate secretarial/advisory retainers, or stage-wise appearance and pleading fees for litigation and arbitration. A transparent written scope of work is shared after the initial conflict check.'
