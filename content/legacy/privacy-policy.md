# Legacy Privacy Policy Record — Zodiac Legal

Zodiac Legal respects the privacy of visitors to `zodiaclegal.com` and complies with applicable Indian data protection statutes, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## Core Principles Preserved
1. **Voluntary Submission:** Personal data (Name, Email, Telephone Number, Organization, and Inquiry Details) is collected only when voluntarily submitted via the consultation inquiry form or direct email correspondence.
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Data is processed strictly to respond to the user's specific inquiry, perform conflict-of-interest checks, and schedule requested consultations.
3. **No Third-Party Sale:** Zodiac Legal never sells, rents, or trades personal information to marketing agencies or third-party data brokers.
4. **Confidentiality Warning:** Submitting unilateral information via the website form prior to a formal engagement letter does not by itself establish an advocate-client privilege.
